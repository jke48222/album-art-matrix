Words worth
playing with.

Five word games, each with its own tactile board, focused controls, and a matching composition on the wall. Paths, categories, clues and progress stay connected across both surfaces.

Actual native app screens: before on the left, after on the right. Each pair uses the same scale and preserves the complete screenshot. Games use deterministic production puzzle fixtures and real moves.

These captures verify rendering and API states. They do not certify touch interaction, real listening, or the physical LEDs’ colour.
