01 · Synthesis

Case

Index's cassette with Console's face. Four satin aluminium sides at the honest depth Index argued for, and a charcoal wool front plate instead of bare LEDs. The picture sits 15 mm back in a black anodised well — a shallow Vitrine — behind smoked acrylic, inside the reveal from the walnut design, standing 3 mm proud of the glass the way Console's bezel did. Fully enclosed and vented, from the boxed build.

The mains module is on the underside at the right, 165 mm off centre, where Index put it: reach under with your right hand, and the cord drops down the side.

This is the one that belongs next to the brushed-metal lamp and the white turntable. It leaves out anything warm on purpose.

Hearth

Soft's broken rectangle tamed to a square with 70 mm chamfers, in the walnut design's veneer with its brass keys moved from the mitres to the chamfers, where they read as four short bright lines as you pass. The picture is 40 mm down a well lined in Vitrine's oatmeal wool, behind smoked acrylic in a brass bezel. A reliquary in a wooden case.

Soft's black power shoe hangs from the bottom edge 140 mm left of centre, the one precise interruption in a soft form, and the switch faces the floor inside it.

The deepest of the three and the most furniture-like. It is also the one that most wants real veneer rather than paint: the chamfers show the grain changing direction, and paint has no grain to change.

03 · New

Strap

A black lacquered shell with 50 mm corners, hung from a brass hook on a 40 mm saddle-leather strap that comes down the wall, over the top edge and 25 mm down the face, where a brass plate and two studs hold it. The picture flush behind smoked acrylic inside a 4 mm brass reveal.

The reference is Jacques Adnet's mirror for Hermès, 1950: a pane of glass hung from a leather strap on a brass fitting, the strap in proportion to the pane, one of the first objects to put leather against glass. Its lesson is that the mounting can be the design. Everything else on this wall hides how it hangs. This one makes hanging the whole idea, and it is the only design here whose silhouette includes the wall above it.

The palette is Adnet's — black, tan, brass — and it is also the room's: the black cubby unit, the brushed-metal lamp, the warm floor. The mains module is in the bottom edge at the right, in a brass escutcheon, found by feel.

It costs a stud. The hook carries about 6 kg and has to be in one; four 25 mm pads keep the shell off the plaster so the halo has room. A belt and a hook are the whole mounting bill.

Honest gaps

What a render cannot settle

Whether thin aluminium sheet on a wooden rail stays flat enough for Case's sides, which is the finish that makes or breaks it. Whether veneer around a 70 mm chamfer can be edge-banded cleanly or wants a mitred solid corner. And, for Strap, whether the shell hangs plumb from a single centre point with the supply off to one side inside it — a question of where the mass is, answered by hanging it before finishing it.
