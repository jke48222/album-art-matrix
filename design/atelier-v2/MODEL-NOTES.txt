TESSERA / ATELIER / DESIGN STUDY 02

DESIGN INTENT
A dark sculpted wall object with a continuous satin-bronze perimeter, recessed optical face and a tapered rear. No front fasteners. The cabinet, materials and dimensions below are new design proposals, not assertions about purchased hardware. This is a visual and packaging design study, not a fabrication release or tested electrical/thermal assembly.

DESIGNED GEOMETRY
536 x 536 x 116 mm body. Outer corner radius 12 mm at the face. Rear envelope 492 mm, tapering through 498 mm to the broad front shoulder. 1.2 mm front bronze reveal. 508 x 508 x 3 mm proposed cover-glass envelope. Full 480 mm active aperture; no deliberate pixel cropping. Replaceable 494 x 494 x 2 mm diffuser envelope with 8 mm nominal separation from the LED emission plane. A processor must confirm available glass grade, coating, edge work and tint; the render does not certify transmission, haze, brightness, off-state black or glare performance. ACRYLITE Satinice is a researched diffuser sample candidate, not a proven optical solution for this wall.

Mounting uses the purchased OOK 12-inch cleat with designed 16 mm spacers. Published installed cleat projection is 1/8 inch (3.175 mm); combined nominal projection is 19.175 mm. Cleat cross-section height and wall fixing pattern require the physical part; capacity of the complete enclosure/anchors has not been calculated. Two rear ventilation openings are 340 x 22 mm before guard bars. Airflow, noise and enclosure temperatures are not simulated or measured.

VERIFIED SOURCE GEOMETRY
1. Raspberry Pi 5: official RP-010083-CA-1 STEP, converted to triangle meshes without geometry scaling; 2,689 original solids. This is Raspberry Pi's reference model, not a scan of the user's 1 GB board. Materials are assigned for visualization; exact population/finish can vary. Manufacturer drawing itself says reference-only, not production data.
2. Adafruit 6358: official 3MF, 138 mesh objects, original geometry and colours, no scale changes. Board mesh measures 65 x 30.7 x 1.57 mm. Complete CAD z range -5.54 to 10.47 mm. This corrects the earlier 65 x 56 stand-in.
3. Frienda B084Q4W1PW: seller drawing 51 x 5 x 23 mm overall; listing says approximately 12 mm exposed pins, 2.54 mm pitch and 0.6 mm pins. Nominal plastic-body height is therefore 11 mm by subtraction. Model seats the riser onto the Pi's header housing and seats the bonnet's bottom socket face onto the riser's top, per user instruction. Bonnet pins are aligned to its CAD socket centre y=26.49. No deliberate floating air gap is inserted. The nominal stack uses rounded seller dimensions and mating-face contact; it is not a measured assembly or proof of internal socket insertion depth.
4. Waveshare: official P2_5 DWG specifies 159.8 +/-0.1 mm rear shell, 12 mm shell depth, 2121 LED suitability and eight M3 mounting points. CAD extraction gives M3 centres relative to panel centre: (+/-45,+/-73) and (+/-73,+/-45) mm. Model uses four of the eight per panel. Shell ribs are simplified from the drawing; the actual panel connector locations and driver-chip population are not established by that shell drawing. Whole module thickness is not claimed to be 12 mm. Nominal tiled panel pitch remains 160 mm; LED pitch 2.5 mm, 192 x 192 = 36,864 LED faces.
5. Mean Well LRS-350-5: 215 x 115 x 30 mm, manufacturer's mechanical tolerance +/-1 mm. Fan centre follows the drawing (47.45 mm from right, 36.7 mm from top). Nine terminals are shown in manufacturer order. Perforation contours and sheet-metal details are reconstructed visually; the model is not a supplier STEP.
6. RVBOATPAT: exact seller dimension image supplies 5.4 in length, 0.9 in width, 4.7 in mounting span, 3.8 in plate length and 0.4 in screw pitch. The two rows of six screws and separate cover follow the product photographs. Vertical base/cover details remain photo reconstructions, not a production drawing.
7. Adafruit 3367: published overall 22.2 x 18.3 x 7 mm. A short USB extension is proposed to move it away from the controller bay. Recognition/acoustic performance inside this cabinet is not tested.
8. Adafruit 4162 STEMMA QT PCB: 1.0 x 0.7 in, mounting pitch 0.8 x 0.5 in from official fab print. Sensor is positioned facing upward in the proposed chassis. Confirm actual received PCB revision and cable access.
9. Antrader B081VD1NNT: seller overall 50 x 30 x 30 mm. Cutout, snap tabs and insertion clearance are not specified. The inlet remains inside a provisional service-pod zone, not a released panel cutout.
10. Ametherm SL2210005: datasheet gives max diameter 22 mm, max thickness 5 mm, nominal lead pitch 7.8 mm. The guard is a designed reserved volume. The circuit and thermal separation are not approved by this model.

UNRESOLVED FIT
The collection 'U Unresolved fit' is explicitly segregated. Nilight NI-FH01 body dimensions are not stated by the linked listing or pasted text. The 5 x 1 x 3 inch entry cannot establish the rubber body geometry; 12 inches is the wire loop and 19.1 x 18.5 x 5.1 mm describes the compatible fuse. Its dark body and tether are visual photo forms only. Do not manufacture its saddles from those forms. Module HUB75/power connector positions likewise need rear photographs or measurement. No geometry in this collection is asserted accurate. Proposed clamp tabs, screws and brackets need final mechanical detailing and tolerance review.

SYSTEM CHOICES
Retain the nine panels, Pi, fully seated Frienda riser/bonnet, Active Cooler, LRS-350-5, bus bars, nine inline 10 A fuse holders, owned wire and IEC inlet. Keep the 27 W USB-C Pi supply external with its original function; no new mains branch or replacement regulator is assumed. TCS34725 modules remain external calibration tools. UCA202 remains an optional external audio interface rather than permanently consuming wall enclosure volume. No parked backplane PCB is used. Cable curves describe proposed routes, not wiring instructions, a pinout or verified electrical clearances. Existing inrush/current claims from the repository are not adopted as tested results.
